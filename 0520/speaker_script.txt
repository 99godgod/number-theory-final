╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 3  ·  RSA/ECC 基本原理                                     ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
先快速回顧現行公鑰系統的數學基礎。RSA 的安全性建立在整數分解上：公
鑰是兩個大質數的乘積 N = pq 加上指數 e，私鑰是模 φ(N) 的逆元 d。已
知最佳的古典演算法是 General Number Field Sieve，複雜度 exp(O(n^(1/3)
(log n)^(2/3)))，亞指數時間。ECC 的安全性建立在橢圓曲線離散對數問題
ECDLP 上：公鑰是 Q = kP，私鑰是純量 k；最佳古典演算法 Pollard rho 是
平方根時間 O(√p)。這兩個系統是今日 HTTPS、TLS 1.3、SSH 的數學基礎，
也是 Shor 演算法最直接的攻擊目標。

[English]
A quick review of the mathematical foundations of current public-key
systems. RSA's security rests on integer factorisation: the public
key is a product of two large primes N equals pq together with an
exponent e; the private key is the modular inverse d of e modulo
φ(N). The best classical algorithm is the General Number Field
Sieve, with complexity exp of O of n to the one-third times log n
to the two-thirds — sub-exponential time. ECC's security rests on
the elliptic curve discrete logarithm problem: the public key is Q
equals kP, the secret is the scalar k; the best classical algorithm
is Pollard rho in square-root time O of root p. Both systems
underlie HTTPS, TLS 1.3, and SSH today — and both are Shor's most
direct targets.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 4  ·  Shor 演算法（1994）                                  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
1994 年，Peter Shor 把整數分解與 ECDLP 同時化為「週期尋找問題」，並
用量子傅立葉轉換在多項式時間內求解。以 RSA 為例，核心函數是 f(x) =
aˣ mod N；若能找出最小正整數 ω 使得 a^ω ≡ 1 (mod N)，則 gcd(a^(ω/2)
± 1, N) 很大機率分解 N。QFT 的作用是把週期函數的疊加態峰值放大，在
O((log N)³) 的量子門電路複雜度內讀出週期 ω。對 ECDLP，類似地把群元
素的離散對數歸約至加法群上的週期問題，同樣多項式可解。結論：一旦容
錯量子電腦造出，RSA、Diffie-Hellman、ECDLP 三者同時失效。

[English]
In 1994, Shor reduced both integer factorisation and ECDLP to a
period-finding problem, solved by the quantum Fourier transform in
polynomial time. For RSA, the core function is f of x equals a to
the x modulo N; finding the smallest positive ω such that a to the
ω is congruent to 1 modulo N allows computing gcd of a to the omega
over two plus or minus 1 with N, which factors N with high
probability. The QFT amplifies the peak of a superposition of a
periodic function and reads out ω in O of (log N) cubed quantum
gate complexity. For ECDLP, the discrete logarithm is similarly
reduced to a period problem on an abelian group, again polynomial
time. Conclusion: once a fault-tolerant quantum computer is built,
RSA, Diffie–Hellman, and ECDLP all fall simultaneously.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 5  ·  Period Finding 尋找週期                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
更仔細看週期問題。週期函數定義是：存在最小正整數 ω 使 f(x + ω) =
f(x) 對所有 x 成立。QFT 在量子電腦上的角色是把 |x⟩ 的均勻疊加映射
為 |k⟩ 的疊加，當 k 是 N/ω 的倍數時振幅被加強，量測後以高機率得到
ω 的倍數，再用輾轉相除法還原 ω。RSA 的歸約：整數分解 → 找乘法群週
期；ECDLP 的歸約：離散對數 → 找加法群週期。這兩條歸約路徑讓 Shor
得以統一攻破兩者。格上的 SVP 目前沒有類似的週期結構，是現有量子演
算法無法利用的原因之一。

[English]
A closer look at the period problem. A periodic function f has a
minimum positive integer ω such that f of x plus ω equals f of x
for all x. The QFT's role on a quantum computer is to map a uniform
superposition of |x⟩ states to |k⟩ states whose amplitudes are
reinforced when k is a multiple of N over ω; measuring with high
probability yields a multiple of ω, from which ω is recovered by
Euclid's algorithm. RSA reduction: integer factorisation maps to
finding a period in the multiplicative group. ECDLP reduction:
discrete logarithm maps to finding a period in an additive group.
These two reduction paths let Shor attack both with a single
algorithm. Lattice SVP currently has no analogous periodic
structure — one reason existing quantum algorithms cannot exploit it.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 6  ·  格密碼學的登場                                       ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
格密碼學的奠基工作來自三個里程碑。1996 年，Ajtai 給出首個 worst-case
到 average-case 的歸約：解隨機格實例等效於解任意最壞情形的 SVP，安全
保證遠強於 RSA；2005 年，Regev 提出 Learning With Errors，並給出量子
歸約把 worst-case SVP 連到 average-case LWE 安全性；2010 年，
Lyubashevsky、Peikert、Regev 三人結合代數數論提出 Ring-LWE，用分圓整
數環把公鑰從 O(n²) 壓縮到 O(n)、乘法從 O(n²) 加速到 O(n log n)。最
關鍵的事實：SVPγ 對最佳已知量子演算法的複雜度仍是 2^Θ(n)，也就是
指數難，沒有人知道如何做得更快。

[English]
Lattice cryptography rests on three milestones. In 1996, Ajtai gave
the first worst-case to average-case reduction: solving random
lattice instances is as hard as worst-case SVP — a guarantee far
stronger than RSA's. In 2005, Regev introduced Learning With Errors
with a quantum reduction connecting worst-case SVP to average-case
LWE security. In 2010, Lyubashevsky, Peikert, and Regev combined
this with algebraic number theory to propose Ring-LWE, using the
cyclotomic ring of integers to compress public keys from O of n
squared to O of n and to accelerate multiplication from O of n
squared to O of n log n. The critical fact: for the best known
quantum algorithms, SVPγ still requires 2 to the Theta of n time —
exponential — and no one knows how to do better.

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 9  ·  格的定義                                             ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
什麼是格？取 R^n 中 n 個線性獨立的向量 b₁, …, bₙ，所有整數線性組合
構成的集合 Λ = ⊕ᵢ ℤbᵢ 就是一個格 — R^n 的離散加法子群。基底矩陣 B
把這些向量並排成列（或行），覆積定義為 det(B) 的絕對值 |det B|，是基
底選擇無關的不變量，幾何上代表基本胞的體積。相繼極小 λᵢ(Λ) 定義為
「能在 i 維格點子集中線性生成 i 維空間的最小半徑」。最重要的是 λ₁(Λ)，
即最短非零格向量的歐式範數。

[English]
What is a lattice? Take n linearly independent vectors b₁ through
bₙ in Rⁿ; the set of all integer linear combinations Λ equals the
direct sum of ℤbᵢ is a lattice — a discrete additive subgroup of
Rⁿ. The basis matrix B places these vectors as columns, and the
covolume is defined as the absolute value of det B — an invariant
independent of the chosen basis, representing the volume of a
fundamental cell. The successive minimum λᵢ(Λ) is the smallest
radius such that the ball of that radius intersects the lattice in
i linearly independent vectors. Most important is λ₁(Λ): the
Euclidean length of the shortest nonzero lattice vector.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 10  ·  Good Basis vs Bad Basis                             ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
同一個格可以用截然不同的基底描述，這就是 SVP 困難性的直觀來源。用好
的基底 — 幾乎正交、向量短 — 最短向量顯而易見，直接看出來就是某個基
向量或其短線性組合。用壞的基底 — 高度歪斜、向量長 — 最短向量被隱藏
在成千上萬個格點之中，任何簡單的四捨五入都會跑到錯誤的格點。密碼學
的設計正是利用這個非對稱性：合法擁有者持有好基底可以高效解密；攻擊
者只拿得到壞基底，無法有效尋找最短向量。

[English]
The same lattice can be described by very different bases — this is
the intuitive source of SVP hardness. With a good basis — nearly
orthogonal, short vectors — the shortest vector is immediately
visible as one of the basis vectors or a short combination. With a
bad basis — highly skewed, long vectors — the shortest vector is
buried among exponentially many lattice points, and any naive
rounding leads to the wrong lattice point. Cryptographic design
exploits this asymmetry: the legitimate holder of a good basis can
decrypt efficiently; the attacker who only has a bad basis cannot
find the shortest vector efficiently.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 11  ·  SVP 定義                                            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Shortest Vector Problem，最短向量問題。精確版 SVP：給定基底 B，找一
個非零格向量 v ∈ Λ 使 ‖v‖₂ = λ₁(Λ)。近似版 SVP_γ：找 v 使 ‖v‖₂ ≤
γ · λ₁(Λ)；近似因子 γ ≥ 1 越大，問題越容易。決策版 GapSVP_{γ}：區
分「λ₁ ≤ 1」與「λ₁ > γ」兩種情形。複雜度：精確 SVP 是 NP-hard（在
隨機歸約下）；GapSVP_{√n} 在 NP ∩ coNP 中；對常數 γ，目前最佳古典
與量子演算法均需 2^Θ(n)。

[English]
The Shortest Vector Problem. The exact version SVP: given a basis B,
find a nonzero lattice vector v in Λ such that the Euclidean norm
of v equals λ₁(Λ). The approximate version SVP_γ: find v with norm
at most γ times λ₁(Λ); larger approximation factor γ makes the
problem easier. The decision version GapSVP_γ: distinguish between
λ₁ at most 1 and λ₁ greater than γ. Complexity: exact SVP is
NP-hard under randomised reductions; GapSVP_{√n} lies in NP
intersection coNP; for constant γ, the best known classical and
quantum algorithms all require 2 to the Theta of n time.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 12  ·  CVP 定義                                            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Closest Vector Problem，最近向量問題。給定基底 B 與目標點 y ∈ R^n（y
不一定在格中），找格向量 v ∈ Λ 使 ‖y − v‖₂ = dist(y, Λ)，其中距離
定義為 min_{x ∈ Λ} ‖y − x‖₂。特例 BDD（Bounded Distance Decoding）：
當 dist(y, Λ) < λ₁/2 時，最近向量唯一且可被部分演算法高效求解。難度
關係：CVP ≥ SVP，也就是說 CVP 至少和 SVP 一樣困難；精確 CVP 是
NP-hard，且在 NPH 下甚至無法做到因子 2^(log n)^(1-ε) 的近似。CVP 在
密碼學中的角色：LWE 解碼等價於 BDD，後量子安全直接依賴 CVP 困難。

[English]
The Closest Vector Problem. Given a basis B and a target point y in
Rⁿ, not necessarily on the lattice, find the lattice vector v in Λ
minimising the Euclidean distance ‖y minus v‖₂, where the distance
is min over x in Λ of ‖y minus x‖₂. The special case BDD, Bounded
Distance Decoding, arises when dist(y, Λ) is less than λ₁ over two:
the nearest vector is then unique and some algorithms can find it
efficiently. Hardness relation: CVP is at least as hard as SVP;
exact CVP is NP-hard, and under NPH it cannot even be approximated
within a factor of 2 to the log n to the one minus epsilon. CVP's
role in cryptography: LWE decryption is equivalent to BDD, so
post-quantum security directly depends on CVP hardness.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 13  ·  為什麼困難？                                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
直觀地理解 SVP/CVP 的困難性。第一，基底歪斜時四捨五入失效：把目標點
投影到基底座標後四捨五入，歪斜基底的投影座標會有很大的誤差，直接取
最近整數幾乎必然落到錯誤的格點。第二，好基底本身難找：給一個任意基
底，找一組又短又正交的等價基底，這本身就是格約簡問題，沒有多項式時
間算法能做到任意精度。第三，指數維度的搜索空間：n 維格中基本胞有
det(Λ) 的體積，候選格點數在 det(Λ) 的尺度下是指數多，窮舉不可行。第
四，已知最佳量子演算法（Sieving）複雜度 2^Θ(n)，量子平行化只能節省
常數因子，無法突破指數障礙。

[English]
An intuitive explanation for why SVP and CVP are hard. First, naive
rounding fails under a skewed basis: projecting the target onto the
basis coordinates and rounding to the nearest integer accumulates
large errors when the basis is skewed — the result almost always
lands on the wrong lattice point. Second, finding a good basis is
itself hard: given an arbitrary basis, finding an equivalent one
with short, nearly orthogonal vectors is the lattice reduction
problem, for which no polynomial-time algorithm achieves arbitrary
precision. Third, the search space is exponential: the fundamental
cell has volume det(Λ), and the number of candidate lattice points
scales exponentially with the dimension. Fourth, the best known
quantum algorithm (Sieving) requires 2 to the Theta of n; quantum
parallelism saves only constant factors and cannot break the
exponential barrier.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 14  ·  複雜度景觀                                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
這張表快速彙整各演算法的複雜度。LLL：多項式時間 O(n⁶ log³ B)，近似
因子 2^((n-1)/2)；BKZ-β：時間 2^O(β log β)，近似因子 β^(n/2β)；精確
Sieve（古典）：時間與空間均 2^(0.292n+o(n))；精確 Sieve（量子）：時間
2^(0.265n+o(n))；暴力枚舉：時間 2^O(n log n)。結論：沒有次指數時間演
算法；近似求解與精確求解之間有清晰的效率分界。這張表是 Section 3 的
路線圖。

[English]
A quick summary table of algorithm complexities. LLL: polynomial
time O of n to the sixth times log cubed B, approximation factor
2 to the (n minus 1) over 2. BKZ-β: time 2 to the O of β log β,
approximation factor β to the n over 2β. Exact Sieve classical:
both time and space 2 to the 0.292n plus o of n. Exact Sieve
quantum: time 2 to the 0.265n plus o of n. Brute-force enumeration:
time 2 to the O of n log n. Conclusion: no sub-exponential time
algorithm is known; there is a clear efficiency gap between
approximate and exact solving. This table is the road map for
Section 3.

for CVP.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 17  ·  Gram-Schmidt 正交化                                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
所有格約簡演算法的基礎是 Gram-Schmidt 正交化（GSO）。對基底 b₁, …, bₙ，
遞推定義 b*ᵢ = bᵢ − ∑_{j<i} μᵢⱼ b*ⱼ，其中 μᵢⱼ = ⟨bᵢ, b*ⱼ⟩ / ‖b*ⱼ‖²
是 Gram-Schmidt 係數。注意兩個關鍵點：第一，GSO 的輸出 b*ᵢ 不是格基
— 係數 μ 不是整數，所以 {b*ᵢ} 構成的格與 Λ 不同；第二，det(Λ) = ∏ᵢ
‖b*ᵢ‖，GSO 範數的乘積等於覆積，是不變量。GSO 的幾何意義是把每個基
向量投影到前面所有向量的正交補空間，量測這個投影長度。後面的 LLL
和 BKZ 都以 GSO 為基本工具，通過控制 μᵢⱼ 的大小和 ‖b*ᵢ‖ 的遞減速
率來保證輸出品質。

[English]
The foundation of all lattice reduction algorithms is the
Gram-Schmidt orthogonalisation, GSO. For basis b₁ through bₙ,
define recursively: b-star-i equals b-i minus the sum over j less
than i of μ-ij times b-star-j, where μ-ij equals the inner product
of b-i and b-star-j divided by the squared norm of b-star-j.
Two key observations: first, the GSO output b-star-i is not a
lattice basis — the coefficients μ are not integers, so the span of
{b-star-i} is not the same lattice Λ; second, det(Λ) equals the
product of the norms ‖b-star-i‖ — the product of GSO norms equals
the covolume, an invariant. Geometrically, each GSO vector is the
component of a basis vector orthogonal to the span of all preceding
vectors. LLL and BKZ both use GSO as the core tool, maintaining
quality by controlling the sizes of the μ-ij coefficients and the
rate of decrease of the ‖b-star-i‖ values.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 18  ·  LLL 演算法                                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
1982 年，Lenstra、Lenstra、Lovász 三人提出 LLL 演算法，是第一個多項式
時間的格約簡演算法。LLL 維護兩個條件：（1）Size condition：|μᵢⱼ| ≤ 1/2
對所有 i > j；（2）Lovász condition：‖b*ᵢ‖² ≥ (δ − μ²_{i,i-1}) ‖b*_{i-1}‖²，
通常取 δ = 3/4。演算法核心是：每步若某個相鄰對不滿足 Lovász condition，
就交換 bᵢ 和 b_{i-1} 並更新 GSO；否則對 bᵢ 做 size reduce，往左移動。
輸出保證：第一個基向量 ‖b₁‖ ≤ 2^((n-1)/2) λ₁(Λ)，即近似因子為指數。
時間複雜度：O(n⁶ log³ B)，多項式時間。歷史意義：1982 年發表後立刻
用來破解 Merkle-Hellman knapsack 密碼系統，這是格密碼分析的第一場勝
利。

[English]
In 1982, Lenstra, Lenstra, and Lovász proposed the LLL algorithm,
the first polynomial-time lattice reduction algorithm. LLL maintains
two conditions: first, the size condition, that the absolute value
of μ-ij is at most one half for all i greater than j; second, the
Lovász condition, that the squared norm of b-star-i is at least
delta minus μ squared of i comma i minus one, times the squared
norm of b-star of i minus one, typically with delta set to three
quarters. The core step: if adjacent pair i and i minus one violates
the Lovász condition, swap b-i and b of i minus one and update the
GSO; otherwise size-reduce b-i and move left. Output guarantee: the
first basis vector satisfies ‖b₁‖ at most 2 to the (n minus one)
over two times λ₁(Λ) — an exponential approximation factor. Time
complexity: O of n to the sixth times log cubed B, polynomial time.
Historical significance: immediately after publication in 1982, LLL
was used to break the Merkle–Hellman knapsack cryptosystem — the
first victory for lattice cryptanalysis.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 19  ·  LLL 的威力與限制                                    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
LLL 的優點是多項式時間，實作快，對許多應用已足夠。缺點是近似因子
2^((n-1)/2) 隨維度指數成長：n = 100 時近似因子約 2^50，也就是說輸出
的第一個向量可能是最短向量的 10^15 倍長 — 對密碼分析不夠精確。直觀
地理解 LLL 的限制：它只做「局部最佳化」，每步只比較兩個相鄰基向量，
對整體結構的改進有限。一個好的格約簡演算法應該要比較更大的窗口，這
就是 BKZ 的出發點。此外，LLL 輸出的基底品質依賴格的「本徵複雜度」，
對惡意選擇的基底可能需要很多次迭代。

[English]
LLL's strengths are polynomial time and fast implementation, already
sufficient for many applications. Its weakness is the approximation
factor 2 to the (n minus one) over two, which grows exponentially
with dimension: at n equals 100, the factor is roughly 2 to the 50,
meaning the output vector can be 10 to the 15 times longer than the
true shortest vector — insufficient for cryptanalysis. Intuitively,
LLL's limitation is that it performs only local optimisation,
comparing two adjacent basis vectors per step, achieving limited
global improvement. A better reduction algorithm should look at a
larger window — this is the starting point for BKZ. Additionally,
the quality of LLL's output depends on the intrinsic complexity of
the lattice; adversarially chosen bases may require many iterations.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 20  ·  BKZ 演算法                                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Block Korkine-Zolotarev，簡稱 BKZ，是目前格密碼分析的實務主流。核心
思路：取大小為 β 的「滑動視窗」，在每個 β 維子格上精確求解 SVP（呼
叫 SVP oracle），再把結果插回完整基底。參數 β 稱為「block size」或「塊
大小」。當 β = 2 時退化為 LLL；β 越大，輸出越好，時間越長。輸出品質：
‖b₁‖ ≈ β^(n/(2β)) λ₁(Λ)，近似因子遠優於 LLL。時間複雜度：2^O(β log β)，
精確求解每個子窗口是瓶頸。實務上 BKZ-20 到 BKZ-40 是密碼分析的主
流選擇，能有效攻擊 n = 400–600 的格。最新變體 BKZ 2.0 加入了提前終
止、預處理、隨機局部搜索，在實務中更高效。

[English]
Block Korkine-Zolotarev, BKZ, is the practical workhorse of lattice
cryptanalysis. Core idea: slide a window of size β along the basis,
call an exact SVP oracle on each β-dimensional projected sublattice,
and insert the result back into the full basis. The parameter β is
called the block size. At β equals 2, BKZ degenerates to LLL; larger
β gives better output at higher cost. Output quality: ‖b₁‖ is
approximately β to the n over 2β times λ₁(Λ) — far better than LLL.
Time complexity: 2 to the O of β log β, with the exact SVP oracle
call being the bottleneck. In practice, BKZ-20 to BKZ-40 is the
standard cryptanalytic range, effectively attacking lattices of
dimension n between 400 and 600. The newer variant BKZ 2.0 adds
early termination, preprocessing, and randomised local search for
better practical performance.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 21  ·  Sieving — 精確求解 SVP                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Sieving 是目前精確求解 SVP 最快的一類演算法。基本思路是「篩法」：
從格中抽大量隨機格點，對每對格點 v, w，若 ‖v − w‖ < ‖v‖，則用 v −
w 替換 v；重複直到大多數格點都很短，最短向量出現在其中。2002 年，
Ajtai、Kumar、Sivakumar（AKS）給出第一個 2^O(n) 時間的 SVP 演算法，
空間複雜度也是 2^O(n)。GaussSieve（2010）是最廣泛實作的版本，時間與
空間均約 2^(0.292n)。量子版 Sieving（Laarhoven 2015）結合量子尋找演算
法，時間降至 2^(0.265n)，但空間仍需 2^O(n)，是主要瓶頸。Sieving 的
核心限制：空間需求 2^Θ(n) 在 n = 500 以上時記憶體需求超過 10^40 bit，
無法實作；因此實務中 BKZ 加 Sieving 混合才是主流。

[English]
Sieving is the fastest known class of algorithms for exact SVP.
The basic idea: sample a large collection of random lattice points;
for each pair v, w, if ‖v minus w‖ is less than ‖v‖, replace v
with v minus w; repeat until most vectors are short and the shortest
vector appears in the collection. In 2002, Ajtai, Kumar, and
Sivakumar gave the first 2 to the O of n time algorithm for SVP,
with space also 2 to the O of n. GaussSieve from 2010 is the most
widely implemented variant, with both time and space approximately
2 to the 0.292n. Quantum Sieving by Laarhoven in 2015, combining
quantum search, reduces the time to 2 to the 0.265n, but space
remains 2 to the O of n — the main bottleneck. The fundamental
limitation of Sieving: memory requirement 2 to the Theta of n
exceeds 10 to the 40 bits for n above 500, making direct
implementation infeasible; in practice, BKZ combined with Sieving
for the inner SVP oracle is the standard.

║  Slide 22  ·  求解策略比較表                                      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
快速瀏覽這張比較表。LLL：多項式時間，指數近似因子，密碼分析上限約
n = 150；BKZ-β：時間 2^O(β log β)，近似因子 β^(n/2β)，實務密碼分析
n ≤ 600；Enumeration 枚舉：時間 2^O(n log n)，精確或高品質近似，空間

精確 SVP，空間 2^O(n) 是瓶頸。選擇依維度與目標：低維用 LLL，中高維
用 BKZ + Sieving oracle，高精度低維用 Enumeration。這個比較直接決定

[English]
A quick walk through the comparison table. LLL: polynomial time,

equals 150. BKZ-β: time 2 to the O of β log β, approximation factor

Enumeration: time 2 to the O of n log n, exact or high-quality

space 2 to the O of n is the bottleneck. The choice depends on

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 23  ·  CVP 求解：Babai 演算法                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
CVP 的兩個主要啟發式解法都由 Babai（1986）提出。第一個是 Babai
Rounding（四捨五入法）：計算 c = B⁻¹y，對每個分量取最近整數 ĉᵢ =
round(cᵢ)，輸出 Bĉ。在好基底下可行，但在歪斜基底下誤差累積，容易
失敗。第二個是 Babai Nearest Plane（最近平面法）：從最後一個基向量開
始，逐維投影並取最近的「平面」，遞推到第一維。這等價於 BDD 的精確
解：若 dist(y, Λ) < λ₁ / (2√n)，Nearest Plane 保證找到最近格點。比
Rounding 更強，因為每一步的誤差被投影消除，不會累積。這兩個方法依
賴好基底，故在密碼分析中常與 LLL/BKZ 合用：先約簡基底，再跑 Babai。

[English]
The two main heuristic CVP solvers were both proposed by Babai in
1986. First, Babai Rounding: compute c equals B inverse times y,
round each component to the nearest integer ĉᵢ, and output B times
ĉ. This works under a good basis but fails under a skewed basis
because rounding errors accumulate. Second, Babai Nearest Plane:
starting from the last basis vector, project onto successive
hyperplanes and pick the nearest one, recursing down to dimension
one. This is equivalent to exact BDD solving: if dist(y, Λ) is less
than λ₁ over 2 times square root of n, Nearest Plane is guaranteed
to find the closest lattice vector. Nearest Plane is strictly
stronger than Rounding because each projection eliminates the
accumulated error. Both methods require a good basis, so in practice
they are combined with LLL or BKZ first: reduce the basis, then run
Babai.

║  Slide 24  ·  BDD → LWE 連結                                      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
這張投影片是 Section 3 與 Section 2 之間最關鍵的橋樑：LWE 的解密其
實就是 BDD 求解。LWE 的樣本是 b = As + e，其中 A 是矩陣，s 是秘密，
e 是小雜訊向量。把 A 的列看成格 Λ(A) 的基底，則 b = As + e 是一個
「距格點 As 距離 ‖e‖ 很小」的點。解密 LWE 就等價於：在格 Λ(A) 中，
找距離 b 最近的格點 As，也就是 BDD。因此：若能解 BDD → 能破解 LWE；
SVP 困難 → LWE 安全的歸約鏈成立。這也說明為什麼攻擊 Kyber 等 LWE
系統的演算法（BKZ + Sieving）和求解 SVP/BDD 的演算法是同一套工具。

[English]
This slide bridges Section 3 and Section 2: LWE decryption is
exactly BDD solving. An LWE sample is b equals A times s plus e,
where A is a matrix, s is the secret, and e is a small noise vector.
Viewing the columns of A as the basis of a lattice Λ(A), the vector
b equals A s plus e is a point at distance ‖e‖ — small — from the
lattice point A s. Decrypting LWE is therefore equivalent to finding
in Λ(A) the lattice point A s closest to b — that is BDD. The
implication: if one can solve BDD, one can break LWE; the chain SVP
hardness implies LWE security holds. This also explains why the
algorithms used to attack LWE-based systems like Kyber — BKZ plus
Sieving — are the same tools used to solve SVP and BDD.

[中文]
基於以上的演算法景觀，如何選擇安全參數？核心工具是「BKZ 安全估算模

全強度 λ bit 要求最小 β 使 2^(0.292β) ≥ 2^λ，即 β ≥ λ / 0.292。
Kyber-512（FIPS 203 Level 1）：n = 256，q = 3329，攻擊 BKZ-β 的最小
β ≈ 405，對應計算複雜度 2^118 — 超過 AES-128 的 2^128 安全目標的合理

間（影響安全與效率）、誤差分布 χ 的標準差決定解密可靠度（影響正確
率）。三角平衡是後量子方案設計的核心挑戰。

[English]
Given the algorithmic landscape, how do we choose security
parameters? The core tool is the BKZ security estimation model:
BKZ-β attack with Sieving oracle requires time 2 to the 0.292β
plus o of β. For a target security of λ bits, the minimum β must
satisfy 2 to the 0.292β at least 2 to the λ, so β at least λ
divided by 0.292. For Kyber-512, FIPS 203 Level 1: n equals 256,
q equals 3329; the minimum BKZ-β has β approximately 405,
corresponding to attack complexity 2 to the 118 — well above the
AES-128 reference of 2 to the 128. The three parameters that must
be balanced: n determines the lattice dimension and thus security;
q determines the noise range and affects both security and
efficiency; the standard deviation of the error distribution χ
governs decryption correctness. This three-way balance is the
central challenge of designing post-quantum schemes.

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 28  ·  Minkowski 第一定理                                  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Minkowski 第一定理（凸體定理）：設 Λ 是 R^n 中的格，S ⊆ R^n 是中心
對稱凸體，且體積滿足 vol(S) > 2ⁿ det(Λ)，則 S 必然含有至少一個非零
格點。三個關鍵詞解釋：中心對稱 — 對所有 x ∈ S，−x 也在 S 中；凸體
— S 是有界閉集，且任意兩點的連線段都含在 S 中；體積條件 vol(S) > 2ⁿ
det(Λ) — 注意必須是嚴格大於，等號時不保證有格點。這個定理把「幾何體
積」與「格的算術性質」連在一起，是幾何數論最核心的工具之一。

[English]
Minkowski's first theorem, the convex body theorem: let Λ be a
lattice in Rⁿ, and let S be a centrally symmetric convex body in Rⁿ
with volume strictly greater than 2 to the n times det(Λ); then S
must contain at least one nonzero lattice point. Three keywords:
centrally symmetric — for every x in S, minus x is also in S; convex
body — S is a bounded closed set and the line segment between any
two points of S lies in S; volume condition vol(S) strictly greater
than 2 to the n times det(Λ) — strict inequality is essential, the
conclusion may fail at equality. This theorem links geometric volume
with arithmetic properties of the lattice, and is one of the central
tools in the geometry of numbers.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 29  ·  SVP 上界推導                                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
現在把 Minkowski 定理套到具體的立方體上，推導 λ₁ 的上界。取中心對稱
立方體 Cᵣ = [−r, r]ⁿ，體積為 (2r)ⁿ。Minkowski 條件：(2r)ⁿ > 2ⁿ det(Λ)，
化簡得 r > det(Λ)^(1/n)。結論：只要 r > det(Λ)^(1/n)，Cᵣ 中就含非零
格點 v。由於 v ∈ Cᵣ，‖v‖∞ ≤ r。範數不等式：‖v‖₂ ≤ √n · ‖v‖∞ ≤ √n · r。
讓 r 從上方逼近 det(Λ)^(1/n)，得 λ₁(Λ) ≤ √n · det(Λ)^(1/n)。這個上
界連結了「幾何上的覆積」（det(Λ)）與「算術上的最短向量長度」（λ₁）。
更精確版本用 Hermite 常數 γₙ：λ₁ ≤ √γₙ · det(Λ)^(1/n)，γₙ 的漸近
上界是 n / (πe) + O(1)。

[English]
We now apply Minkowski's theorem to a specific cube to derive an
upper bound on λ₁. Take the centrally symmetric cube Cᵣ equals the
product of closed intervals [-r, r], with volume (2r) to the n.
Minkowski's condition: (2r) to the n strictly greater than 2 to the
n times det(Λ), simplifying to r strictly greater than det(Λ) to
the one over n. Conclusion: for r exceeding det(Λ) to the one over n,
the cube Cᵣ contains a nonzero lattice point v. Since v is in Cᵣ,
its infinity norm ‖v‖∞ is at most r. The standard norm inequality
gives ‖v‖₂ at most square root of n times ‖v‖∞, hence at most
square root of n times r. Letting r decrease to det(Λ) to the one
over n, we obtain λ₁(Λ) at most square root of n times det(Λ) to
the one over n. This bound links the geometric covolume det(Λ) with
the arithmetic shortest vector length λ₁. A tighter version uses
the Hermite constant γₙ: λ₁ is at most square root of γₙ times
det(Λ) to the one over n, with γₙ asymptotically at most n over
πe plus lower order terms.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 30  ·  定理的意義                                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Minkowski 定理的意義遠超過一個簡單的上界。它說明了以下幾件事：格點
「一定存在」— 在覆積的合理尺度內，格中必有不太長的向量；這個存在性
是非構造性的 — 定理告訴我們格點存在，但不告訴我們如何找到它，這正
是 SVP 困難性的幾何根源；上界 √n · det(Λ)^(1/n) 是嚴格的 — 存在格
（如 D_n 型格）使等號幾乎成立；定理把「格的幾何（體積）」與「格的算
術（最短向量）」連成一個等式，是格密碼安全分析的核心工具。在密碼學
應用中，Minkowski 界幫助我們評估「攻擊者能找到多短的向量」，從而決
定安全參數是否足夠。

[English]
Minkowski's theorem is far more than a simple upper bound. It
establishes several things: lattice points must exist — within the
natural scale of the covolume, the lattice always contains vectors
that are not too long; the existence is non-constructive — the
theorem guarantees a lattice point exists but does not say how to
find it, which is precisely the geometric root of SVP hardness; the
bound square root of n times det(Λ) to the one over n is tight —
lattices such as the D_n root lattice nearly achieve equality; and
the theorem bridges lattice geometry, volume, and lattice
arithmetic, shortest vectors, in a single inequality, making it the
central analytical tool for lattice cryptography security analysis.
In cryptographic applications, the Minkowski bound helps estimate
how short a vector an attacker can hope to find, and thus whether
the security parameters are adequate.

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 32  ·  NIST 後量子標準                                     ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
2024 年 8 月，NIST 公布首批後量子密碼標準，格密碼佔主導地位。FIPS
203（ML-KEM，基於 Kyber）：金鑰封裝機制，數學基礎為 Module-LWE；
Kyber-512 公鑰 800 byte，密文 768 byte；安全強度 2^118，對應 BKZ-405。
FIPS 204（ML-DSA，基於 Dilithium）：數位簽章，數學基礎為 Module-LWE
與 Module-SIS；公鑰 1312 byte，簽章 2420 byte。兩者的安全性都直接歸
約到格上的 SVP/CVP 困難性。Google、Cloudflare 已在 TLS 1.3 混合部署
ML-KEM；Apple 宣布 iMessage 支援 PQ3，基礎同為格密碼。SVP/CVP 困難
性，不再只是數學問題，已成為全球網路基礎建設的核心。

[English]
In August 2024, NIST published the first post-quantum standards,
with lattice cryptography in a dominant position. FIPS 203, ML-KEM
based on Kyber: a key encapsulation mechanism whose mathematical
basis is Module-LWE; Kyber-512 has an 800-byte public key and a
768-byte ciphertext; security strength 2 to the 118, corresponding
to BKZ-405. FIPS 204, ML-DSA based on Dilithium: a digital signature
scheme whose basis is Module-LWE and Module-SIS; public key 1312
bytes, signature 2420 bytes. Both schemes' security reduces directly
to SVP and CVP hardness on lattices. Google and Cloudflare have
already deployed hybrid ML-KEM in TLS 1.3; Apple announced PQ3 for
iMessage, also built on lattice cryptography. SVP and CVP hardness
is no longer merely a mathematical question — it has become the
foundation of global internet infrastructure.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 33  ·  開放問題                                            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
格密碼學有幾個核心的開放問題值得關注。第一，SVP 的量子下界：目前最
佳量子演算法是 2^(0.265n)，但沒有人證明量子下界是指數，量子電腦是否
能做到次指數 SVP 仍然未知。第二，Ideal-SVP vs SVP：Ring-LWE 的安全
性歸約到理想格上的 SVP（Ideal-SVP），而理想格有額外的代數結構，是否
比任意格的 SVP 更容易？目前這個問題是開放的，若 Ideal-SVP 容易解，
Ring-LWE 就不安全。第三，古典 worst-case 歸約：Regev 的 LWE 歸約是量
子的，純古典的 worst-case → average-case 歸約至今未找到。第四，格密碼
vs 其他 PQC：相較於 NIST 也標準化的 SPHINCS+（雜湊簽章）和基於碼的
密碼，格密碼的效率優勢和安全理解的深度有何比較？

[English]
Several core open problems in lattice cryptography deserve attention.
First, the quantum lower bound for SVP: the best known quantum
algorithm runs in 2 to the 0.265n, but no one has proven an
exponential quantum lower bound — whether a quantum computer can
solve SVP in sub-exponential time remains unknown. Second,
Ideal-SVP versus SVP: Ring-LWE's security reduces to SVP on ideal
lattices, which carry extra algebraic structure; whether Ideal-SVP
is easier than SVP on arbitrary lattices is open — if it is, Ring-LWE
is insecure. Third, classical worst-case reduction: Regev's LWE
reduction is quantum; a purely classical worst-case to average-case
reduction has not been found. Fourth, lattice cryptography versus
other post-quantum candidates: compared with NIST-standardised
SPHINCS+ and code-based cryptography, how do lattice schemes compare
in efficiency and depth of security understanding?
