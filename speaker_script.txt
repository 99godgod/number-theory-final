╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 3  ·  RSA — 整數分解                                       ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
先看 RSA。1977 年提出，是最廣泛部署的公鑰系統之一。公鑰是兩個大質
數的乘積 N 加上指數 e；私鑰是模 φ(N) 的逆元 d。安全性建立在「整數
分解問題」上：給你 N，要還原 p 與 q 是困難的。古典最佳演算法是
General Number Field Sieve，亞指數時間，目前 RSA-2048 仍被認為安
全。今天的 HTTPS、銀行系統、SSH 全部仰賴這個假設。

[English]
First, RSA. Proposed in 1977, it is one of the most widely deployed
public-key systems. The public key is a product of two large primes
N together with an exponent e; the private key is the inverse of e
modulo φ(N). Its security rests on the integer factoring problem:
given N, recovering p and q is believed hard. The best classical
algorithm is the General Number Field Sieve, sub-exponential in
log N. RSA-2048 is still considered secure today, and HTTPS, banking
systems, and SSH all depend on this assumption.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 4  ·  ECC — ECDLP                                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
第二個是橢圓曲線密碼 ECC。1985 年由 Miller 與 Koblitz 各自提出。把
有限體上的橢圓曲線 y² = x³ + ax + b 上的點看成阿貝爾群，公鑰是 Q =
kP，私鑰是純量 k。安全性建基於橢圓曲線離散對數問題 ECDLP。優點是
金鑰小：256-bit ECC 安全強度約等於 3072-bit RSA。古典最佳演算法是
Pollard rho，平方根時間。ECDSA 用於 TLS 憑證、區塊鏈簽章。

[English]
Second, Elliptic Curve Cryptography, proposed independently by
Miller and Koblitz in 1985. The points on a finite-field elliptic
curve y² = x³ + ax + b form an abelian group; the public key is
Q = kP, the secret is the scalar k. Its security rests on the
elliptic curve discrete logarithm problem. The advantage is short
keys: a 256-bit ECC key offers security comparable to 3072-bit
RSA. The best classical algorithm is Pollard rho, with square-root
running time. ECDSA underlies TLS certificates and blockchain
signatures.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 5  ·  Shor 演算法 (1994)                                   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
1994 年，Peter Shor 在 FOCS 發表一個量子演算法，把整數分解和離散對
數同時化為「求週期」問題，再用量子傅立葉轉換以多項式時間求出。後果
非常嚴重：對 RSA-2048，古典 GNFS 是亞指數時間，量子 Shor 只需要約
log N 立方時間；對 ECDLP，古典 Pollard rho 是平方根，量子 Shor 同樣
是 log n 立方。也就是說，一旦我們造出夠大的容錯量子電腦，RSA、
Diffie-Hellman、ECC 三者會「同時」失效，沒有一個能撐住。

[English]
In 1994, Peter Shor presented a quantum algorithm at FOCS that
reduces both integer factoring and discrete logarithm to a period-
finding problem, and solves them in quantum polynomial time using
the quantum Fourier transform. The consequences are severe. For
RSA-2048, classical GNFS is sub-exponential, while quantum Shor
runs in roughly cubic time in log N. For ECDLP, the classical
square-root running time becomes cubic in log n on a quantum
computer. In other words, once a sufficiently large fault-tolerant
quantum computer exists, RSA, Diffie–Hellman, and ECC will fall
together — none of them survives.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 6  ·  Harvest Now, Decrypt Later                           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
威脅其實不需要等到量子電腦造好。攻擊者今天就可以攔截並保存密文，等
到未來量子電腦上線後再解密 — 這就是「harvest now, decrypt later」，
先攔截後解密。對於生命週期長的機密 — 醫療紀錄、外交電文、商業機密、
身分認證金鑰 — 這個威脅已經存在。NIST 預估 2030 到 2035 年量子電
腦可能達到破解規模，因此後量子密碼的部署必須提前 — 這是現在進行式。

[English]
The threat does not actually require a working quantum computer
today. An adversary may intercept and store ciphertexts now, then
decrypt them once a quantum computer becomes available — this is
called "harvest now, decrypt later." For long-lived secrets such as
medical records, diplomatic cables, trade secrets, and identity
keys, this threat is already present. NIST estimates that quantum
computers capable of breaking these schemes may appear between
2030 and 2035. Post-quantum cryptography must therefore be
deployed in advance — this is happening right now.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 7  ·  解方：格密碼學                                       ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
這就帶到格密碼學。1996 年 Ajtai 給出第一個 worst-case 到 average-
case 的歸約，把格上某些「困難問題」轉成隨機實例的安全保證；2005 年
Regev 提出 Learning With Errors，是第一個有效的後量子公鑰加密候選；
2010 年 Lyubashevsky、Peikert、Regev 三人結合代數數論，把整數向量
換成數環中的元素，提出 Ring-LWE。Ring-LWE 把公鑰大小從 n 平方縮為
線性 n、把乘法從 n 平方加速到 n log n — 兼得安全與效率。

[English]
This leads to lattice-based cryptography. In 1996, Ajtai gave the
first worst-case to average-case reduction for lattice problems,
turning hard lattice problems into security guarantees for random
instances. In 2005, Regev introduced Learning With Errors, the
first efficient post-quantum public-key encryption candidate. In
2010, Lyubashevsky, Peikert, and Regev combined this with
algebraic number theory: replacing integer vectors with elements
of a number ring, they proposed Ring-LWE. Ring-LWE shrinks public
keys from quadratic to linear in n, and accelerates multiplication
from n² to n log n — gaining both security and efficiency.

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 10  ·  格 (Lattice) 的定義                                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
什麼是格？把實數空間 R^n 中 n 個線性獨立的向量 b1 到 bn 取整數線性
組合，所得的集合 L 就是一個格 — 它是 R^n 的離散加法子群。基底矩陣
B 把這些向量並排，覆積定義為 |det B|，這是與基底選擇無關的不變量，
幾何上代表一個基本胞的體積。相繼極小 λ_i 是「能裝進球裡的格點還能
維持 i 維生成」的最小半徑。最關鍵的是 λ_1：第一個非零最短向量的長
度。

[English]
What is a lattice? Take n linearly independent vectors b₁ through
bₙ in Rⁿ, and form all integer linear combinations: this discrete
additive subgroup is a lattice L. The basis matrix B places these
vectors as columns, and the covolume is defined as the absolute
value of det B — an invariant independent of the chosen basis,
representing the volume of a fundamental cell. The successive
minima λ_i is the smallest radius such that a ball of that radius
intersected with the lattice still spans an i-dimensional subspace.
The most important is λ_1: the length of the shortest nonzero
lattice vector.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 11  ·  格上的困難問題                                       ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
格上有三大困難問題。第一個是 SVP — 最短向量問題：找一個非零格點
v，長度不超過 γ 倍的 λ_1。第二個是 GapSVP，決策版本：判斷 λ_1 是
小於等於 1，還是大於 γ。第三個是 BDD — 有界距離解碼：給一個離格
不太遠的點 t，找最近的格點。在小近似因子 γ 下，目前最佳的古典演算
法和量子演算法都需要 2 的 n 次方時間，沒有人知道怎麼做更快。實作上
最廣泛使用的是 BKZ 結合 sieving 演算法。

[English]
There are three central hard problems on lattices. First, SVP, the
shortest vector problem: find a nonzero lattice vector v of length
at most γ times λ_1. Second, GapSVP, the decision variant:
distinguish whether λ_1 is at most one or greater than γ. Third,
BDD, bounded distance decoding: given a target t close to the
lattice, find the nearest lattice point. At small approximation
factor γ, the best classical and quantum algorithms both require
exponential time, two to the n. In practice, BKZ combined with
sieving is the standard approach.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 12  ·  Minkowski 第一定理                                  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
現在進入 Minkowski 第一定理 — 這是幾何數論的奠基結果。陳述是這樣：
若 S 是 R^n 中的中心對稱凸體，且體積大於 2^n 倍的覆積，那麼 S 必含
非零格點。三個關鍵詞：中心對稱表示對任何 x 屬於 S，-x 也在 S 中；
凸體表示任兩點線段含於 S，且 S 是有界閉集；體積條件 vol(S) > 2^n
det(L) 是必要的臨界條件 — 等號時不一定成立。下一頁我們把它套到一個
具體的立方體上，推得最短向量的上界。

[English]
Now Minkowski's first theorem — the cornerstone of the geometry of
numbers. Statement: if S is a centrally symmetric convex body in
Rⁿ with volume strictly greater than 2ⁿ times the lattice covolume,
then S must contain a nonzero lattice point. Three keywords:
centrally symmetric means x in S implies -x in S; convex body means
the line segment between any two points of S lies in S, and S is
bounded and closed; the volume condition is the critical threshold —
strict inequality is essential. Next slide: we apply this to a
specific cube to derive the shortest-vector bound.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 13  ·  推導 SVP 上界 — Step 1                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
取中心對稱立方體 C_r 等於 [-r, r]^n，每邊長 2r，體積等於 (2r) 的 n
次方。Minkowski 定理需要的條件是：vol(C_r) > 2^n det(L)，也就是
(2r)^n > 2^n det(L)，等價於 r > det(L) 的 n 分之一次方。所以只要 r
取得比 det(L) 的 n 分之一次方稍微大一點點，立方體 C_r 就一定含有一
個非零格點 v。注意這個 v 屬於立方體，所以它的「無窮範數」— 也就是
最大座標絕對值 — 不超過 r。

[English]
Take the centred cube C_r equal to the closed interval [-r, r] in
each coordinate; each side has length 2r, and the volume equals
(2r) raised to n. Minkowski's condition becomes (2r)^n strictly
greater than 2^n det(L), which simplifies to r strictly greater
than det(L) to the one over n. Therefore, whenever r exceeds this
threshold even slightly, the cube C_r must contain a nonzero
lattice vector v. Notice that v lies in the cube, so its
infinity-norm — the maximum coordinate in absolute value — is at
most r.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 14  ·  推導 SVP 上界 — Step 2 + 結論                       ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
接下來用一個標準的範數不等式：對任何 R^n 中的向量 v，「歐式範數」不
超過根號 n 倍的「無窮範數」。把 Step 1 的 ‖v‖∞ ≤ r 代入，得到 ‖v‖_2
≤ √n · r。這對任何滿足 r > det(L) 的 n 分之一次方都成立。讓 r 從上
方逼近 det(L) 的 n 分之一次方，就得到結論：λ_1(L) ≤ √n · det(L) 的
n 分之一次方。這就是 Minkowski 給的最短向量長度上界。Hermite 常數
γ_n 可以給出更緊的版本，γ_n 的漸近上界是 2n 除以 πe，但 √n 已經夠
我們在 Ring-LWE 的安全分析中使用。

[English]
Next, a standard norm inequality: for any vector v in Rⁿ, the
Euclidean norm is at most the square root of n times the infinity
norm. Substituting from Step 1, the infinity norm of v is at most
r, so its Euclidean norm is at most square root of n times r. This
holds for every r exceeding det(L) to the one over n. Letting r
descend to that threshold, we obtain the conclusion: λ_1 of L is
at most square root of n times det(L) to the one over n. This is
Minkowski's upper bound on the shortest vector. The Hermite
constant γ_n yields a tighter version, with asymptotic upper bound
two n over π e, but the simple square-root-n bound suffices for
our Ring-LWE security analysis.

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 17  ·  一般 n-th cyclotomic field                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
先看一般情形。對任意正整數 n，本原 n 次單位根 ζ_n 定義為 e 的 2πi/n
次方 — 在複平面上，它是單位圓上的 n 等分點。它滿足 ζ_n^n = 1，但對
所有小於 n 的正整數 k，ζ_n^k 不為 1。第 n 個分圓多項式 Φ_n(x) 定
義為所有「跟 n 互質」的本原單位根的乘積：x − ζ_n^k，跑遍所有
gcd(k,n) = 1 的 k。這是一個首一、整係數、在有理數上不可約的多項式，
次數是 Euler totient 函數 φ(n)。

[English]
Start with the general case. For any positive integer n, the
primitive n-th root of unity ζ_n is defined as e to the two pi i
over n — geometrically, an n-th equidistant point on the unit
circle. It satisfies ζ_n^n equals one, but for every positive
integer k less than n, ζ_n^k is not one. The n-th cyclotomic
polynomial Φ_n of x is defined as the product of x minus ζ_n^k,
ranging over all k coprime to n. This is a monic polynomial with
integer coefficients, irreducible over the rationals, of degree
equal to Euler's totient φ(n).


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 18  ·  分圓多項式範例                                      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
看幾個小例子體會一下。Φ_1 = x − 1，因為唯一的「本原 1 次單位根」就
是 1。Φ_2 = x + 1，本原是 −1。Φ_3 = x² + x + 1，是次數 2 的不可約
多項式。Φ_4 = x² + 1，注意這是高斯整數環的最小多項式。Φ_5 是次數 4
的多項式 x^4 + x^3 + x^2 + x + 1。Φ_6 = x² − x + 1。最關鍵的是
Φ_8 = x^4 + 1，再來是 Φ_12 = x^4 − x² + 1。注意一個漂亮的事實：當
n 是 2 的次方時，Φ_{2^k}(x) = x^(2^(k−1)) + 1 — 形式特別簡單，這
正是 Ring-LWE 實作首選 power-of-two cyclotomic 的原因。

[English]
Some small examples. Φ_1 equals x minus 1, since the only primitive
first root of unity is 1. Φ_2 equals x plus 1, with primitive root
minus 1. Φ_3 equals x squared plus x plus 1, an irreducible
quadratic. Φ_4 equals x squared plus 1 — the minimal polynomial of
the Gaussian integers. Φ_5 has degree 4, x to the fourth plus all
lower terms. Φ_6 equals x squared minus x plus 1. Most importantly,
Φ_8 equals x to the fourth plus 1, and Φ_12 equals x to the fourth
minus x squared plus 1. Note a beautiful fact: when n is a power of
2, Φ_{2^k} of x equals x to the (2^{k-1}) plus 1 — an especially
clean binomial form. This is precisely why power-of-two cyclotomics
are the implementations of choice for Ring-LWE.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 19  ·  分圓域 K 與整數環 O_K                               ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
第 n 個分圓域 K = Q(ζ_n) 是由 ζ_n 所生成的有理數體擴張，其度數是
φ(n)。它的整數環 O_K 等於 Z[ζ_n] — 注意這是一個非平凡結果，並非
所有的「域擴張」整數環都等於「相鄰」整數所生成的環，但對分圓域成
立。這個整數環是 Dedekind domain — Course Obj. 1, 2 — 性質是：
Noetherian、整封閉、Krull 維度 1。最關鍵的性質是每個非零理想可以
唯一分解為質理想的乘積。我們在整數 Z 上失去的「元素的唯一分解」，
在 Dedekind domain 上以「理想的唯一分解」回歸 — 這是代數數論最深
刻的洞見之一。

[English]
The n-th cyclotomic field K equals Q(ζ_n), the field generated by
ζ_n over the rationals, with degree φ(n). Its ring of integers O_K
equals Z[ζ_n] — a non-trivial result, since it is not generally
true that adjoining a generator to Z gives the full ring of
integers, but it does hold for cyclotomic fields. This ring of
integers is a Dedekind domain — Course Objectives one and two —
meaning: Noetherian, integrally closed, Krull dimension one. The
crucial property is that every nonzero ideal admits a unique
factorisation into prime ideals. The unique factorisation of
elements that we lose in general number rings returns at the level
of ideals in a Dedekind domain — one of the deepest insights of
algebraic number theory.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 20  ·  特殊化至 m = 2^k                                    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
從這裡開始我們特殊化到 power-of-two 情形：m = 2^k，k ≥ 2。原因如前
所述，分圓多項式 Φ_m(x) = x^(2^(k-1)) + 1，形式最簡。為了和 Ring-
LWE 文獻一致，從這裡開始用 LPR 的記號慣例：m 代表「分圓指標」，n
記為 φ(m) = 2^(k-1)，代表「域度 [K : Q]」。具體例子：k = 2，m = 4，
n = 2，K = Q(i)；k = 3，m = 8，n = 4；實用尺寸像 k = 10，m = 1024，
n = 512，這就是 Kyber 之類密碼方案的典型維度。

[English]
From here on, we specialise to the power-of-two case: m equals two
to the k, with k at least two. As mentioned, Φ_m of x equals x to
the (2^{k-1}) plus 1 — the simplest form. To stay consistent with
the Ring-LWE literature, we adopt the LPR notational convention
from now on: m denotes the cyclotomic index, while n equals φ(m)
equals 2^{k-1} denotes the field degree [K : Q]. Concrete examples:
k equals 2 gives m equals 4, n equals 2, K equals Q of i; k equals
3 gives m equals 8, n equals 4. A practical size is k equals 10,
m equals 1024, n equals 512 — the typical dimension for schemes
like Kyber.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 21  ·  質理想分裂 (Course Obj. 3)                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
分圓域中的質理想分裂有非常乾淨的描述。對於不整除 m 的有理質數 p，令
f 是 p 在 (Z/m)* 中的乘法階。則 p O_K 在 K 中分解成 g = n / f 個相
異質理想的乘積，每個的 inertia degree 是 f，分歧指標是 1。對於 p =
2 — 這是 m = 2^k 時唯一的分歧質數 — 我們有 2 O_K 等於 (1 − ζ_m)
的 n 次方，是「完全分歧」的。Ring-LWE 的關鍵設計選擇：取 q 為質數
且 q ≡ 1 mod m。這時 f = 1，q 在 K 中「完全分裂」為 n 個相異質理
想；這直接對應到下一節要看的 NTT 結構。

[English]
Prime splitting in cyclotomic fields admits a very clean
description. For a rational prime p not dividing m, let f be the
multiplicative order of p modulo m in (Z/m)*. Then p O_K
factorises into g equals n over f distinct prime ideals in K, each
of inertia degree f and ramification index one. For p equals two —
the unique ramified prime when m is a power of two — we have two
O_K equals one minus ζ_m to the n-th power, which is totally
ramified. The key design choice in Ring-LWE: pick q prime
satisfying q congruent to one modulo m. Then f equals one and q
splits completely into n distinct prime ideals in K — this directly
corresponds to the NTT structure we will see shortly.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 22  ·  Trace, Norm, Discriminant                           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
四個基本不變量：trace 跡是 α 在所有嵌入下的像之和；norm 範數是所有
像之乘積。Different 差理想 d^(-1) 的定義是：所有 α 使得 Tr(α O_K)
落在 Z 中。判別式 Δ_K 等於 different 的範數。對 power-of-two
cyclotomic m = 2^k，可以算出 |Δ_K| = 2 的 n(k − 1) 次方；其平方
根 √|Δ_K| = 2 的 n(k − 1)/2 次方 — 這個量會在下一頁的「理想格覆
積」公式中出現。具體地，m = 8、n = 4、k = 3 時，|Δ_K| = 2^8 =
256，√|Δ_K| = 16。直觀上，判別式測量了「整數環的扭曲程度」：分歧
質數恰是 Δ_K 的質因子。

[English]
Four basic invariants. The trace of α is the sum over all field
embeddings of σ_i of α; the norm is the product over all embeddings.
The different inverse, denoted by a fraktur d to the minus one, is
defined as the set of α such that the trace of α times O_K lies in
Z. The discriminant Δ_K equals the norm of the different. For
power-of-two cyclotomics, the absolute discriminant equals two to
the n times k minus one, and its square root equals two to the n
times k minus one over two. This quantity will appear in the ideal
lattice covolume formula on the next slide. For m equals 8, n
equals 4, k equals 3: the discriminant is 2 to the eighth, equals
256, and its square root is 16. Intuitively, the discriminant
measures how distorted the ring of integers is: the ramified primes
are exactly the prime factors of Δ_K.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 23  ·  Canonical Embedding — 把代數翻譯為幾何              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
現在到了 Section 3 的關鍵時刻：把代數結構翻譯為幾何結構。分圓域是
totally complex，r_1 = 0，r_2 = n/2。標準嵌入 σ 把 K 嵌入到 K
tensor R，再透過複數的實虛分量映到 R^n — 採 Minkowski normalisation，
每個複位用 √2 倍實部與虛部。這個嵌入的關鍵性質是：每一個非零理想
a ⊆ O_K，在 σ 下都成為 R^n 中的一個「滿秩理想格」，其覆積等於
N(a) 乘以 √|Δ_K|。這個公式把「範數」與「覆積」、把「代數」與「幾
何」連在一起 — Ring-LWE 的安全性即建基於此：理想格上的 SVP 仍是
量子困難的。

[English]
Here is the pivotal moment of Section 3: translating algebra into
geometry. Cyclotomic fields are totally complex: r_1 equals zero,
r_2 equals n over two. The canonical embedding σ embeds K into K
tensor R, and via the real and imaginary parts of each complex
embedding, into Rⁿ — under the Minkowski normalisation, each
complex place is scaled by square root two times the real part and
square root two times the imaginary part. The key property of this
embedding: every nonzero ideal a in O_K becomes a full-rank ideal
lattice in Rⁿ under σ, with covolume equal to the norm of a times
the square root of the absolute discriminant. This formula links
norm with covolume, algebra with geometry — and Ring-LWE's
security rests on the fact that SVP on these ideal lattices remains
quantum-hard.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 24  ·  Worked Example：m = 8, n = 4                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
用一個具體例子把 Section 3 串起來。取 m = 8、n = 4，這是最小的非平
凡 power-of-two cyclotomic。分圓多項式 Φ_8 = x^4 + 1；K = Q(ζ_8)；
整數環 O_K = Z[ζ_8]；判別式 |Δ_K| = 256，√|Δ_K| = 16。選 q = 17，
因為 17 ≡ 1 mod 8。則 17 modulo 8 的階 f = 1，所以 g = 4：17 O_K
完全分裂為 4 個相異質理想 q_1 q_2 q_3 q_4。等價地，R_17 := O_K /
17 O_K 同構於 Z_17 的四份直積。這就是 NTT 結構的代數來源 — 我們會
在 Section 4 看到計算上的對應。這個小例子完整地連結了 Course
Objectives 1 到 4，加上 Course Objective 5 的理想格覆積。

[English]
Let us tie Section 3 together with a concrete example. Take m
equals 8, n equals 4 — the smallest non-trivial power-of-two
cyclotomic. The cyclotomic polynomial Φ_8 equals x to the fourth
plus 1; K equals Q of ζ_8; the ring of integers is Z of ζ_8; the
absolute discriminant is 256, and its square root is 16. Pick q
equals 17, since 17 is congruent to 1 modulo 8. The order of 17
modulo 8 is f equals 1, so g equals 4: 17 O_K splits completely
into four distinct prime ideals q_1 q_2 q_3 q_4. Equivalently,
R_17, defined as O_K modulo 17 O_K, is isomorphic to four copies
of Z_17. This is the algebraic source of the NTT structure — we
will see the computational counterpart in Section 4. This small
example fully connects Course Objectives one through four, plus

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 26  ·  LWE 直觀解釋                                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
先給直觀。LWE — Learning With Errors — 直觀地說，是「帶雜訊的線
性方程組求解」。給定樣本 (a_i, b_i)，滿足 b_i ≈ <a_i, s> mod q，
也就是內積關係，但每筆觀測都加了一個小的隨機誤差 e_i。目標是還原
秘密向量 s。如果 e_i = 0 — 沒有雜訊 — 那就是高斯消去法，多項式時
間可解，O(n^3)。但只要加上「小」雜訊，問題就突然變得指數困難 — 包
括量子演算法在內目前都沒有有效解法。雜訊的存在「打破了線性結構」，
這正是格密碼安全性的來源。

[English]
First the intuition. LWE — Learning With Errors — informally
asks: solve a noisy linear system. Given samples a_i comma b_i
where b_i is approximately the inner product of a_i and s modulo
q, but each observation is perturbed by a small random error e_i,
recover the secret vector s. If all e_i are zero — no noise —
this is Gaussian elimination, polynomial-time, O of n cubed. But
once we add even small noise, the problem suddenly becomes
exponentially hard — and no efficient quantum algorithm is known
either. The presence of noise breaks the linear structure, and
this is precisely the source of lattice cryptography's security.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 27  ·  LWE 形式定義                                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
形式定義。固定維度 n、模數 q、誤差分布 χ — 通常取離散高斯，寬度
αq，α 小於 1 除以根號 n。固定秘密 s 屬於 Z_q^n。每一個 LWE 樣本是
一對 (a, <a, s> + e mod q)，其中 a 從 Z_q^n 上的均勻分佈抽取，e 從
χ 抽取。Search-LWE 問題：給很多樣本，還原秘密 s。Decision-LWE 問題：
區分這些樣本和均勻隨機。標準歸約告訴我們 Search 與 Decision 在多項
式時間下等價。

[English]
The formal definition. Fix dimension n, modulus q, and an error
distribution χ — typically discrete Gaussian of width α q with α
less than one over root n. Fix a secret s in Z_q to the n. An LWE
sample is a pair: a, together with the inner product of a and s
plus e modulo q, where a is uniform over Z_q to the n and e is
drawn from χ. Search-LWE asks: given many samples, recover s.
Decision-LWE asks: distinguish these samples from uniform. A
standard reduction shows search and decision are polynomially
equivalent.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 28  ·  Regev 量子歸約                                      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Regev 在 2005 年給出最關鍵的安全歸約：解隨機 LWE 實例至少和解任意格
上的 worst-case GapSVP 與 SIVP 一樣困難 — 近似因子 γ = Õ(n / α)。這
個歸約的特殊之處在於：把「最壞情形」的困難性轉化為「平均情形」的安
全性 — 這在密碼學中很罕見。歸約是「量子的」 — 它用到了量子傅立葉轉
換 — 古典歸約至今仍是開放問題。意義是：要破解任何隨機產生的 LWE 實
例，攻擊者必須能解任何「最壞」的格上 SVP — 這是非常強的安全保證。但
LWE 有個顯著缺點：公鑰矩陣 A 大小是 O(n^2)、乘法 O(n^2)。下一頁的
Ring-LWE 就是為了解決這個效率瓶頸而生。

[English]
In 2005, Regev gave the central security reduction: solving random
LWE instances is at least as hard as solving worst-case GapSVP and
SIVP on arbitrary lattices, with approximation factor γ equal to
Õ of n over α. This reduction is remarkable in turning worst-case
hardness into average-case security — a rare guarantee in
cryptography. The reduction is quantum — it uses the quantum
Fourier transform — and a classical version remains open. The
implication: any attacker who can solve a random LWE instance must
also be able to solve every worst-case lattice SVP. The drawback,
however, is significant: the public key matrix A has size O of n
squared, with O of n squared multiplication. Ring-LWE, on the next
slide, is born to address exactly this efficiency bottleneck.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 29  ·  Ring-LWE — 提升至代數整數環                         ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Ring-LWE 由 Lyubashevsky、Peikert、Regev 三人於 2010 年提出。把
Z^n 換成分圓整數環 R = O_K = Z[x] / Φ_m(x) — 對 power-of-two 即
Z[x] / (x^n + 1) — 並令 R_q := R / qR。codifferent R^∨ 是 different
理想的反 — 對應 Course Obj. 4。Ring-LWE 樣本是一對 (a, a · s + e
mod qR^∨)，其中 a 從 R_q 抽，e 是 K_R 上的高斯。秘密 s 在 R_q^∨
中。對 power-of-two 情形，R^∨ = (1/n) R 是純量倍，dual 退化為簡單
的縮放，得到「simplified Ring-LWE」 — 這是實作中常用的版本。安全歸
約：Ring-LWE 至少和理想格上的 approx-SVP 一樣困難（量子歸約）。

[English]
Ring-LWE was introduced by Lyubashevsky, Peikert, and Regev in
2010. Replace Zⁿ by the cyclotomic ring of integers R equals O_K
equals Z of x modulo Φ_m of x — for power-of-two cyclotomics, this
is Z of x modulo x to the n plus 1 — and define R_q as R modulo
q R. The codifferent R-vee is the inverse of the different ideal —
corresponding to Course Objective four. A Ring-LWE sample is a
pair: a, together with a times s plus e modulo q R-vee, where a is
uniform on R_q and e is Gaussian on K_R. The secret s lives in
R_q-vee. For power-of-two cyclotomics, R-vee equals one over n
times R, a scalar dilation; the dual collapses to a simple
rescaling, yielding the simplified Ring-LWE used in practice. The
security reduction: Ring-LWE is at least as hard as approximate
SVP on ideal lattices — a quantum reduction.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 30  ·  質理想分裂的角色 — NTT 的代數來源                   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
這是 Section 3 與 Section 4 連結的關鍵 slide。選 q 為質數且 q ≡ 1
mod m — 此時分圓多項式 Φ_m(x) 在 Z_q 中分裂為 n 個相異一次因子 (x
− ω_i)。透過中國剩餘定理，R_q = Z[x] / (Φ_m(x), q) 同構於 n 個 Z_q
的直積。這個同構，就是 Number-Theoretic Transform（NTT），可以在
O(n log n) 時間內計算 — 類比 FFT 之於普通卷積。乘法在 R_q 中變成
NTT 域上的「逐點相乘」，再透過反向 NTT 回到原表示。幾何意義是：q O_K
完全分裂為 n 個質理想 — 這就是 Course Obj. 3 直接轉化為演算法加速
的具體實例。

[English]
This is the slide that links Sections 3 and 4. Pick q prime with
q congruent to 1 modulo m — then Φ_m of x splits into n distinct
linear factors x minus ω_i over Z_q. By the Chinese Remainder
Theorem, R_q equals Z of x modulo Φ_m of x and q, isomorphic to
the n-fold product of Z_q. This isomorphism is the Number-Theoretic
Transform, NTT, computable in O of n log n — analogous to the FFT
for ordinary convolution. Multiplication in R_q becomes pointwise
multiplication after NTT, then inverse NTT brings the result back.
Geometrically, q times O_K splits completely into n distinct prime
ideals — this is a direct example of Course Objective three turning
into an algorithmic speedup.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 31  ·  Negacyclic 卷積與公鑰大小節省                       ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
因為 Φ_m(x) = x^n + 1，在 R_q 中 x^n 等於 −1，所以乘法是 negacyclic
convolution，不是普通的卷積。具體形式是：(a * b)_k 等於所有 i + j =
k 的 a_i b_j 之和，減去所有 i + j = k + n 的 a_i b_j 之和 — 這個負
號就是「nega」的由來。比較 LWE 和 Ring-LWE：公鑰大小從 O(n^2) 減為
O(n)，省了一個 n 倍；乘法複雜度從 O(n^2) 加速到 O(n log n)，省了
n / log n 倍；安全歸約從「任意格 SVP」變成「理想格 SVP」 — 比較弱，
這是仍然開放的研究議題。Ring-LWE 用「結構」換取「效率」 — 這是現代
密碼學最深刻的設計取捨之一。

[English]
Because Φ_m of x equals x to the n plus 1, in R_q we have x to the
n equivalent to minus 1, so multiplication becomes negacyclic
convolution, not ordinary convolution. Concretely, (a star b)_k
equals the sum of a_i b_j over i plus j equals k, minus the sum
over i plus j equals k plus n — the minus sign is the source of
the name "nega". Comparing LWE and Ring-LWE: public key shrinks
from O of n squared to O of n, a factor n saving; multiplication
accelerates from O of n squared to O of n log n, a factor n over
log n saving; the security reduction moves from arbitrary lattice
SVP to ideal lattice SVP — slightly weaker, and still an open
research question. Ring-LWE trades "structure" for "efficiency" —
one of the most profound design tradeoffs in modern cryptography.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 32  ·  Section 4 小結                                      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
Section 4 小結。LWE 是「帶雜訊的線性方程組求解」；Regev 量子歸約把
worst-case lattice SVP 轉化為 average-case LWE 的安全保證；缺點是公
鑰 O(n^2)。Ring-LWE 把 Z^n 換為分圓整數環，歸約對應到理想格上的
approx-SVP，公鑰縮為 O(n)、乘法 O(n log n) — 透過 NTT。代數結構的具
體貢獻：質理想分裂（Obj. 3）轉化為 CRT 同構，再化為 NTT；codifferent
（Obj. 4）對應 dual ring R^∨，power-of-two 時退化為純量。代數數論
直接決定了 Ring-LWE 的形態與效率。

[English]
Summary of Section 4. LWE is "noisy linear system solving"; Regev's
quantum reduction turns worst-case lattice SVP into an average-case
security guarantee for LWE; the drawback is an O of n squared
public key. Ring-LWE replaces Zⁿ with the cyclotomic ring of
integers, with the reduction now corresponding to approximate SVP
on ideal lattices; the public key shrinks to O of n, and
multiplication accelerates to O of n log n via NTT. Concrete
contributions of algebraic structure: prime ideal splitting,
Objective three, becomes a CRT isomorphism, then NTT; the
codifferent, Objective four, corresponds to the dual ring R-vee,
which collapses to a scalar in the power-of-two case. Algebraic
number theory directly shapes both the form and efficiency of

[中文]
今天日常生活中所有的加密連線 — HTTPS 網站、行動銀行、即時通訊、軟

[English]
Today, every encrypted connection in daily life — HTTPS websites,
mobile banking, instant messaging, software updates, VPNs — relies
on RSA or ECC. The moment a sufficiently capable quantum computer
exists, all of these collapse simultaneously. Combined with the
harvest-now-decrypt-later threat, ciphertexts intercepted today
can be decrypted by future quantum computers — medical records,
diplomatic cables, long-term keys are already exposed. NIST
recommends completing post-quantum migration for critical systems
before 2030; Google, Cloudflare, and Apple have already begun
deploying hybrid ML-KEM in TLS. This transition is happening now.


╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Slide 35  ·  NIST FIPS 203 / 204 (2024)                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

[中文]
2024 年 8 月，經過八年的全球競賽（2016–2024），NIST 公布首批後量子
密碼標準。FIPS 203 是金鑰交換 — 後量子版本的 RSA / Diffie-Hellman 握
手 — 用於兩方建立加密通道前的「秘密握手」；數學基礎是 Ring-LWE 與其
模版本 Module-LWE；公鑰約 800 byte，密文約 1 KB，速度甚至快過 RSA。
FIPS 204 是數位簽章 — 後量子版本的 RSA / ECDSA — 用於 TLS 憑證、軟
體簽章、身分認證；同樣以 Ring-LWE 為數學基礎；簽章約 2.4 KB，公鑰約
1.3 KB。簡單一句話：代數數論加上格幾何，等於你我未來十年的網路安全
基礎。

[English]
In August 2024, after an eight-year global competition from 2016
to 2024, NIST released the first post-quantum standards. FIPS 203
is for key encapsulation — the post-quantum replacement for the
RSA and Diffie–Hellman handshake — used to establish a shared
secret between two parties before opening an encrypted channel;
its mathematical basis is Ring-LWE and its module variant, Module-
LWE; public keys are about 800 bytes, ciphertexts about one
kilobyte, and it actually runs faster than RSA. FIPS 204 is for
digital signatures — the post-quantum replacement for RSA and
ECDSA — used in TLS certificates, software signing, and identity
authentication; signatures are about 2.4 kilobytes, public keys
about 1.3 kilobytes. To put it in one sentence: algebraic number
theory plus lattice geometry equals the security foundation of the
internet for the next decade.
